Outside research, I enjoy many kinds of sports and outdoor activities. Cycling is one of my favorites: I like the rhythm of long rides, the quiet stretches of road, and the chance to explore new places at my own pace.

I also enjoy hiking, running, casual games with friends, and other activities that keep life active and balanced. Some moments are about training, some are about scenery, and some are simply about being outside.
